## 2.1 Prolog Objects

Determine the syntactic correctness of the following Prolog objects. For each correct object, identify its type: atom, number, variable, or structure.

(a) `Diana`
(b) `diana`
(c) `'Diana'`
(d) `_diana`
(e) `'Diana goes south'`
(f) `goes(diana, south)`
(g) `45`
(h) `5(X,Y)`
(i) `+(north, west)`
(j) `three(Black(Cats))`
